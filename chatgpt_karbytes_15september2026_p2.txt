At the same time, the illuminated path is reassuringly explicit. Darkness dominates much of the frame, yet the intended movement line is marked by light. I read that as a small negotiation between uncertainty and continuity: the forest complicates passage without erasing it.

## Overall subjective synthesis

Taken together, p13–p21 enact a steady loss of distance:

1. p13 sees the wooded basin as a unified green mass.

2. p14 surveys it and begins descending.

3. p15 converts the open sidehill into a prolonged transition toward enclosure.

4. p16 and p17 pause to look both ways from within the trees.

5. p18 sustains immersion long enough for the forest’s internal variation to emerge.

6. p19 studies the path’s darker, tangled geometry.

7. p20 looks upward into the luminous canopy.

8. p21 resumes travel without closure.

My strongest impression is that the “Big Green Thing” changes ontological character as the sequence proceeds. From outside, it looks singular and bounded—a green object occupying a basin. Inside, it becomes plural and practically inexhaustible: trunks, slopes, debris, leaf textures, glare, openings, shadows, and turns. The files do not merely show progressively closer views of the same subject; they show how scale and bodily position change what the subject is.
